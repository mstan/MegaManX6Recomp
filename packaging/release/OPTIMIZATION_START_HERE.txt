Mega Man X6 @VERSION@ - Windows x64

Run MegaManX6Recomp.exe, select your own supported disc, then Launch.
OpenBIOS is bundled. Keep the whole folder together. Controller mappings and
features are available in the launcher. Memory cards remain in saves/.
This archive contains no game disc, retail BIOS, personal settings or saves.
Read RELEASE_NOTES.md for validation coverage and known limitations.

Fresh display settings use Widescreen > Fit to screen. Existing choices are
preserved. Turn off Widescreen to test 4:3, or select your original fixed aspect.
For display troubleshooting, enable Recording in Mods > Playtest Diagnostics,
start the game and reproduce in the affected windowed or fullscreen mode.
Press L3 + R3 to mark the moment if you use a controller. Quit normally and
open the session path in diagnostics-location.txt beside the executable. A
session contains starting memory cards and settings, so review it before
sharing. Your disc and BIOS are never copied.

Internal Resolution is enabled at 4x for fresh settings. Choose 1x–32x in Mods; hardware and GPU-memory limits may clamp larger choices. Your saved choices are retained.
