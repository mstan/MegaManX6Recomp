# Mega Man X6 Tweaks attribution and permissions

by [acediez](https://twitter.com/acediez). Project history and upstream credits
are recorded in the [Mega Man X6 Tweaks RHDN project thread](https://www.romhacking.net/forum/index.php?topic=26507.0).

The upstream thread additionally credits DarkSamus933 for voice-clip restoration
research/ASM work and the optional Secret Lab 2 platform, and Gemini for the
font originating in *Blood of Bahamut*. Those credits are retained here through
the same [RHDN project thread](https://www.romhacking.net/forum/index.php?topic=26507.0).

The portrait, mugshot, and palette row remains a re-enablement gate. Its
packages and payloads must not be restored to the public catalog or a release
until direct approval is recorded here. This ledger records permission status;
it does not replace any license terms or grant rights beyond the approvals
received.
